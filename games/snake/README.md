# Snake

Classic arcade snake. Eat the food to grow, and don't run into your own tail.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or open the library's root `index.html` and pick **Snake**.

## Options

- **Speed:** Slow, Normal or Fast. The snake also speeds up a little every 5 pieces of food.
- **Edges:** Solid walls end the game when you hit them. Wrap around lets you leave one side and come back on the other.

Each speed and edge combination keeps its own best score in `localStorage`.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Arrow keys or WASD | Swipe on the board, or use the on-screen arrows |
| Pause / resume | Space or P | Pause button |
